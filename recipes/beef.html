Classic Beef Chili

Classic beef chili is a hearty stew packed with browned ground beef, tender kidney beans, and diced tomatoes simmered in a spiced broth. Ground cumin, chili powder, and smoked paprika deliver warmth and depth without overwhelming heat, making it a reliable comfort food favorite.

Allowing the pot to simmer low and slow gives the beans time to absorb the seasonings while the tomato base thickens naturally. It holds up exceptionally well when made ahead, developing even deeper savory flavor after resting overnight in the refrigerator.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs (680 g) lean ground beef (85/15)
  • 1 medium yellow onion, chopped
  • 1 green bell pepper, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 can (15 oz / 425 g) dark red kidney beans, rinsed and drained
  • 1 can (15 oz / 425 g) black beans, rinsed and drained
  • 1 can (28 oz / 800 g) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 can (14.5 oz / 410 g) petite diced tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 cup beef broth
  • 2.5 tbsp chili powder
  • 1 tbsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp salt, plus more to taste
  • ½ tsp black pepper

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Brown the Beef and Veggies:

    Heat a large Dutch oven or heavy pot over medium-high heat. Add the ground beef, diced onion, and diced green bell pepper. Cook for 6–8 minutes, breaking up the beef with a wooden spoon, until the meat is fully browned and vegetables are softened. Drain excess grease.

  2. Add Aromatics and Spices:

    Stir in the minced garlic, tomato paste, chili powder, ground cumin, smoked paprika, dried oregano, salt, and black pepper. Cook for 1–2 minutes, stirring constantly to toast the spices and deepen the flavor.

  3. Simmer the Chili:

    Pour in the crushed tomatoes, diced tomatoes, beef broth, drained kidney beans, and drained black beans. Stir thoroughly to combine. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover with a lid, and let it simmer for 30–40 minutes, stirring occasionally.

  4. Adjust and Garnish:

    Remove the lid and let simmer uncovered for an additional 10 minutes if you prefer a thicker consistency. Taste and adjust seasoning with additional salt or pepper as needed. Ladle into bowls and serve with shredded cheddar cheese, sour cream, or sliced jalapeños.
